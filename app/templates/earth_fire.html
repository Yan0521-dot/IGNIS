SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Understanding the observations

Thermal anomalies

VIIRS detects thermal signatures that meet the satellite product's detection criteria. These observations should not automatically be classified as confirmed wildfires.

Fire Radiative Power

FRP is a satellite-derived estimate of radiant energy release rate, reported here in megawatts. It is not a direct measurement of ground fire intensity.

Observation limitations

Cloud cover, satellite overpass timing, spatial resolution and detection conditions can affect observations. Acquisition times are UTC.

Research separation

Earth-based observations provide environmental context. They are not spacecraft combustion experiment measurements and are not directly interchangeable with NASA PSI datasets.
