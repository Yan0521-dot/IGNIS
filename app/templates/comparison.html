08 / SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Scientific findings & limitations

OBSERVED · PSI-98

Localised thermal response

SAFFIRE-I Sample 2 recorded a maximum Plume TC3 temperature of 94.403 °C within the 60–150 second analysis window. Its peak was 67.309 °C above the calculated baseline average.

Measured telemetry
EXPERIMENTAL SCOPE · PSI-25

Multiple combustion variables

BASS-II investigates how airflow velocity, oxygen concentration, fuel geometry and flow direction relate to ignition, flame growth, flame spread and extinction limits under microgravity conditions.

NASA investigation metadata
ENGINEERING SIGNIFICANCE

Fire safety research

These investigations contribute different evidence towards understanding material flammability and combustion behaviour in spacecraft environments. Their findings can inform future material selection and spacecraft fire-safety research.

Research relevance
DATA LIMITATION

No direct performance ranking

The available datasets do not establish which investigation had better fire performance. BASS-II numerical telemetry has not yet been integrated, and the experiments are not a controlled head-to-head comparison.

Further evidence required
ⓘ
How IGNIS handles scientific evidence

IGNIS distinguishes measured observations, NASA-documented experimental objectives and engineering interpretations. Temperature peaks do not independently establish sustained combustion or fire duration. Conclusions are restricted to the evidence currently available.
